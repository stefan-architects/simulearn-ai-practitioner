## ☁️ AWS SimuLearn: Exploring Amazon Bedrock Playgrounds

### 📌 Project Overview
The AnyCompany business team wants to find the best AI model for their customer service chat-based AI experience, but they lack a clear evaluation method. They want to thoroughly compare different models that support both text and images during customer conversations to provide optimal customer interactions.

### 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Explore Amazon Bedrock and its role in generative AI development.
- Understand how to interact with foundation models.
- Experiment with model responses using Bedrock Playgrounds.
- Compare model outputs and understand model selection.
- Understand potential business applications of generative AI.
- Develop foundational knowledge of AWS-managed AI services.

### 🧪 Hands-On Activities
- Launch and navigate two Amazon Bedrock playgrounds.
- Analyze and apply model inference parameters through the console.
- Enable compare mode and select two text models to compare responses.
- Create a reusable prompt with variables and save it as a version by using prompt management.

### 🛠️ AWS Services & Technologies
- **Amazon Bedrock:** Managed service for accessing and building generative AI applications using foundation models.
- **Bedrock Playgrounds:** Environment for experimenting with foundation models and prompts.
- **Foundation Models (FMs):** Models used to generate AI-driven responses and content.
- **Generative AI:** Exploration of AI-powered content generation and conversational experiences.
- **AWS Management Console**: Web-based interface for accessing and managing AWS services.

### 💡 Key Takeaways
- Understanding the role of foundation models.
- Exploring generative AI model interactions.
- Evaluating model responses for business applications.
- Understanding how Amazon Bedrock supports generative AI development.

### 🌎 Real-World Applications
- AI-powered customer support.
- Intelligent virtual assistants.
- Business process automation.
- Knowledge retrieval and information assistance.
- Generative AI solutions for enterprise environments.
